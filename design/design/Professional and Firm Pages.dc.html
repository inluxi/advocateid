About

Rahul Nair enrolled with the Bar Council of Kerala in 2008 and worked as a junior to Adv. K. P. Varma on the civil side until 2014. Since then he has practised independently at the High Court of Kerala, mostly in property, land acquisition and service matters.

He lectures on the law of property at Government Law College, Ernakulam, and writes a monthly note on recent High Court judgments.
