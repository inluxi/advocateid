Common questions

What happens if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Your page shows the first items in your order that the lower plan allows. Upgrade again to bring the rest back.

Can I have more than one page?

Yes, up to 3 per mobile number, including firm pages. Each has its own plan.

Is GST included?

Yes.
