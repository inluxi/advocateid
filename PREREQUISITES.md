## 14. Cost breakdown (k3s vs Cloudflare)

| Component | Cloudflare (v6.0) | k3s (v6.1) | Monthly saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compute | Workers: $20–30 | VPS (CloudPE): $11 | $10–20 |
| Database | D1 (included): $0 | Render PostgreSQL FREE: $0 | $0 |
| Storage | R2: $5–10 | Firebase FREE: $0 | $5–10 |
| Networking | Included: $0 | Cloudflare DNS FREE: $0 | $0 |
| **Total** | **~$30/month** | **~$11/month** | **~$19/month (63% cheaper)** |

**At scale (add 2nd VPS node):** ~$22/month (still cheaper than Cloudflare, with better control).
